Everything that arrives, in one place

Your mailboxes, your calendars and the tools to keep both under control.

  • All your mailboxes in one app

    Sign in with Google for Gmail, or with Microsoft for Outlook.com, Hotmail and Microsoft 365. Any other mailbox connects over IMAP and SMTP, with presets for iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail, Zoho and more. A unified inbox shows everything together, coloured by account.

  • A calendar beside your mail

    Switch between mail and calendar in the same window. Google Calendar and Outlook calendars connect when you sign in and sync both ways. CalDAV covers iCloud, Fastmail, Nextcloud and others, and iCal links bring in holidays, fixtures or any shared calendar. View a day, a week, a month, a quarter or the whole year.

  • A calmer inbox

    Smart Inbox keeps mail from people under Important and moves newsletters, receipts and notifications to Other. Snooze a conversation until you can deal with it, schedule a message to send later, take a message back in the seconds after you send it, and save replies you write often as templates.

    It is built for the keyboard: CtrlK opens the command palette, j and k move through your mail, e archives, and every shortcut can be changed.

  • Arabic, right to left

    Each paragraph follows the direction of its first letter, so Arabic reads from the right while English in the same email stays on the left. That holds when you read, when you write, and in subjects and lists. The app itself is available in Arabic too.

    مرحباً سارة، هل يناسبك الاجتماع غداً الساعة العاشرة؟

    Ten works for me. See you then.

  • An assistant, only if you want one

    The AI assistant stays off until you add your own API key from OpenAI, Anthropic or any compatible provider, including models running on your own computer. It can summarise a conversation, draft a reply, and shorten, rephrase or translate what you write. Text goes to the provider you chose, and only when you ask.

  • On your desk and in your pocket

    Desktop apps for Linux, Windows and macOS, and an app for Android phones. Your Camel Mail account keeps your mailbox list, signatures, templates and preferences the same on each of them.

Privacy first

Camel Mail is a mail app, not a mail service. It connects your device straight to the provider that already holds your mail.

  • Google stays on your deviceYour Google sign-in and all Gmail and Google Calendar data stay on your device. They are never sent to, stored on or passed through our servers.
  • No ads, no trackingThere are no ads, analytics or trackers in the app or on this site, and we never sell data.
